# Security Cost & ROI Analysis – On-site Guard vs AI Monitoring

A 5-year cost and ROI model comparing an **overnight on-site guard** with **AI remote video monitoring** (full replacement and a hybrid) for a 300-unit US apartment community with 40 existing cameras. Includes package theft and resident turnover effects.

## Build
- Assumptions sheet with sourced inputs (guard bill rate, monitoring price per camera, setup fees, theft and vacancy costs)
- 5-year model for status quo, full AI and hybrid (4-hour guard + AI), with NPV at 8%
- Sensitivity table: 25 cases across guard rates and camera prices

## Key findings
- AI monitoring costs about **$60,000 a year** vs **$131,400** for a 12-hour guard: 54% lower, or $16.67 vs $36.50 per apartment per month
- Full replacement saves **$82,800 in Year 1**; the $6,400 setup cost pays back in under a month; 5-year NPV about **$351,000**
- The hybrid option still saves about $39,000 in Year 1 (5-year NPV ~$163,000)
- AI stays cheaper up to about $298 per camera per month; savings are positive in all 25 sensitivity cases
- About 86% of savings come from guard labour and 14% from lower losses
